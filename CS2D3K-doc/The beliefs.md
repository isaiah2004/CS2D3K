Below I go over the fundamentals beliefs I hold that will define the design decisions of the product moving forward.

1. There is little to no point reading all the code your agent will write. 
2. Don't reinvent the Wheel. Do not make an agent write code it could have copy pasted or paraphrased. 
3. Do nearly all the thinking. Allow agents to act as though it were your own muscle memory. Make it do the mundane. 
4. Get used to heavy Parallelization of work. 
5. Over test and Over critique and Over validate.
6. Prompt Quality is everything. Write Prompts as though they were code. 
7. Setup fully end to end testing. Test every possible scenario. And setup agentic testing. 
8. Have easy to toggle ultra granular logging. 
9. Write the code in multiple passes focusing on a subset of requirements at a time. 
10. Validate your prompts so you don't have to validate your code. 
11. Your plan should be executable by a sonnet. and opus and fable do the orchestration/checks and stuff like that.
